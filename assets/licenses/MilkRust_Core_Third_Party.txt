## PNG/APNG decoding dependencies

MilkRust uses `png` 0.17.16 from
[image-rs/image-png](https://github.com/image-rs/image-png) for bounded static
PNG and APNG decoding. Its locked Rust deflate backend also distributes
`bitflags` 1.3.2, `cfg-if` 1.0.4, `crc32fast` 1.5.1, `fdeflate` 0.3.7,
`flate2` 1.1.10, `miniz_oxide` 0.8.9 and 0.9.1, `adler2` 2.0.1, and
`simd-adler32` 0.3.10. This distribution elects their MIT terms where an

## MilkDrop 3 saved blend and sprite behavior

The saved blend-pattern equations in `src/milk2_mask.rs` are a clean-room port of
the behavioral implementation and published specification in
[M0Rf30/lactea](https://github.com/M0Rf30/lactea), principally
`crates/lactea/src/engine/md3_blend.rs`,
`crates/lactea/src/engine/blend_patterns.rs`, and `docs/md3.md`. The published
sprite-field behavior in that specification informed the independent parser,
runtime, geometry and renderer in MilkRust. The bounded R8 mask rasterizer and
GPU integration are MilkRust implementations. This notice does not imply
reference-renderer qualification.
